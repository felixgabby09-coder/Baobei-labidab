For My Baba 💌

Dear Baba, One year ago, on October 7th, we officially started our story. Looking back over these past twelve months, every single moment with you has become a memory I treasure deeply. From the late-night talks, the delicious dates, and the random giggles to the simple days where we did nothing at all—having you by my side has made ordinary life extraordinary. This little website is a timeline of our first year together, but it's only the beginning. Thank you for loving me, for being my safe space, and for being my favourite person. Happy 1st Anniversary, Baba. I love you! ♡
